## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium photorealistic macro food photograph for the series El frío tiene textura, vertical 4:5. Input image 1 is the definitive reference for the exact raspberry gelato color, dense fine texture, spatula folds, white ceramic bowl and cool blue-grey setting. Make a NEW close-up camera shot of this same serving, not the full bowl shot again. Frame tightly across the front curved spatula fold with diagonal ridges and delicate scrape marks, raspberry red gelato filling roughly 90% of frame. A narrow cropped arc of the same white bowl at the bottom and a small softly blurred blue-grey background area keep visual continuity. Soft directional daylight from upper left, shadows to lower right, match reference color temperature and raspberry hue. 100mm macro food photography, shallow but controlled depth of field: main fold and fine gelato texture crisply resolved, rear ridges gently softer. Natural freshly served dense fruit gelato, subtle moist highlights, microscopic ice texture without large crystals. No extra berries, garnish, utensils, hands, text, logo or watermark. Avoid plastic gloss, frosting, mousse, meat-like texture, exaggerated holes, melting puddles or oversharpening. Preserve the product identity and restrained editorial lighting of the reference.
